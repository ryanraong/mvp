About us

Thinking Christians, thinking faith

In a city that prizes rigour in every other field, faith is too often left unexamined — treated as a private preference rather than a claim about reality. ACTS exists to close that gap.

We gather students, working professionals, and church members to study the historic Christian faith with intellectual honesty: reading primary sources, engaging serious objections, and learning to answer without defensiveness.

We are interdenominational, lay-led, and committed to serving the local church rather than competing with it.

  • Eager reception

    We come to Scripture expecting to be taught, not merely to have our positions confirmed.

  • Daily examination

    Claims get tested — including our own. Honest questions are a form of respect for the truth.

  • Gentleness and respect

    Apologetics is pastoral before it is polemical. We argue to persuade people, not to win rooms.

  • For the church

    Everything we produce is meant to be carried back into a congregation, a small group, or a friendship.

What we hold

Convictions we work from

We are a broadly evangelical fellowship. These commitments shape our teaching without narrowing it to any one tradition.

Scripture

The Bible is the inspired, trustworthy word of God — the final authority for faith and life, and worth examining closely.

The Triune God

One God eternally existing as Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, who has made himself known in creation, in Christ, and in his word.

Jesus Christ

Fully God and fully man; crucified, buried, and bodily raised — a claim we hold to be historically defensible, not merely devotional.

The Gospel

Salvation by grace through faith in Christ alone, received by repentance and trust, not earned by argument or merit.

Reason as a gift

Sound thinking is not opposed to faith. God is the author of both revelation and the mind that receives it.

The local church

Apologetics belongs in the life of the church. We equip members; we do not replace their congregations.

Programmes

How we gather

Monthly

Berean Nights

An evening built around one hard question — the problem of evil, the reliability of the Gospels, science and Scripture, religious pluralism. A short talk, then extended open floor. No question is out of bounds.

Weekly

Reading Groups

Small groups working slowly through a single text — Scripture, a church father, or a modern work in philosophy of religion. Close reading, shared notes, real discussion.

Termly

Foundations Course

A six-week introduction to Christian apologetics: arguments for God's existence, the historical case for the resurrection, objections to Christian ethics, and how to hold a conversation well.

On request

Church & Campus Workshops

We come to you. Tailored sessions for youth groups, campus fellowships, and adult Sunday classes — pitched to your people and the questions they actually face.
